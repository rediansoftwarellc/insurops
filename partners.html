FAQ

Common Questions

There is no upfront fee to apply or join the partner programme. Commercial models vary by partner type — insurer partners operate on a revenue-share basis, payment partners earn transaction fees, and resellers earn a recurring commission on brokerage subscriptions they onboard.
InsurOps exposes a fully documented REST API (OpenAPI 3.0) with webhook support for real-time events. You'll receive sandbox credentials, a comprehensive developer guide, and a dedicated technical account manager to guide the integration from start to go-live.
InsurOps is currently live in Kenya, India, the UAE, and the United Kingdom, with broker networks active across East Africa, West Africa, South Asia, and the GCC. We're actively expanding and prioritise new markets based on partner demand.
Most partners complete integration and go live within 4–6 weeks of their application being approved. The timeline depends on the complexity of your product catalogue and the availability of your technical team. We assign a dedicated account manager to keep things on track.
For insurer and payment gateway partners, regulatory approval in your target market is a prerequisite to going live. You're welcome to apply and begin the technical integration process in parallel with your regulatory application — we're happy to work with you early.
