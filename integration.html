Platform Capabilities

Built for the Complexity
of Insurance Operations

Insurance workflows are uniquely complex. InsurOps is designed around those specifics — not generic enterprise software adapted for insurance.

Event Streaming

Structured Event Model

Every platform action emits a typed, versioned event. Policy issued, premium received, claim lodged, commission settled — all streamed in real time with full replay capability.

Multi-Tenancy

Isolated Tenant Architecture

Each brokerage runs in a fully isolated environment with dedicated data partitions. No cross-tenant data access. Suitable for enterprise deployments and white-label configurations.

Compliance

Regulator-Ready Audit Trail

Immutable, timestamped logs of every action taken on every record. Exportable in standard formats for regulatory submissions — IRDAI, FCA, IRA Kenya, and UAE Insurance Authority.
